# SEO Quickstart (Google Visibility)

This repo now includes:
- `docs/robots.txt`
- `docs/sitemap.xml`
- canonical + social meta tags on the site and app pages
- JSON-LD structured data on index/app pages

## 1) Verify in Google Search Console
1. Open <https://search.google.com/search-console>
2. Add property:
   - `https://synryzen.github.io/6X-Protocol/` (GitHub Pages), and/or
   - `https://synryzen.com` (main website)
3. Complete verification.

## 2) Submit sitemap
- Submit: `https://synryzen.github.io/6X-Protocol/sitemap.xml`

## 3) Request indexing for core pages
- Home: `https://synryzen.github.io/6X-Protocol/`
- Apps index: `https://synryzen.github.io/6X-Protocol/apps/`
- Each app detail page under `/apps/*.html`

## 4) Keep ranking momentum
- Add at least one new tutorial/release/update page per week.
- Cross-link from `synryzen.com`, App Store listings, and social posts.
- Track clicks/impressions in Search Console and improve low-CTR titles/descriptions.

## 5) If you switch to a custom domain
- Update canonical URLs and sitemap URLs from `synryzen.github.io/6X-Protocol` to your custom domain.
